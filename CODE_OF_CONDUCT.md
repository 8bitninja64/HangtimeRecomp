# Code of conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be welcoming and respectful, criticise ideas rather than people, no harassment,
personal attacks or discrimination, and no sharing of other people's private information.

Additionally, for this project: do not request, offer or link ROMs or other copyrighted game
data anywhere in the project's spaces.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub
(@8bitninja64). Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers may remove content
and restrict or ban participants who break these rules.
